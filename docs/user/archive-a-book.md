## Archive a Book {#archive}

Archiving takes a Book out of the Book menu. Every entry, document and setting stays, and you can unarchive it any time. Agents aren't offered an archived Book when they ask for access.

In the Book's **Settings → Data**, choose **Archive**. Or open **Manage Books** and choose **Archive** beside the Book.

## Bring it back {#unarchive}

Archived Books stay listed in **Manage Books**, marked Archived. Choose **Unarchive** beside one to put it back in the Book menu.

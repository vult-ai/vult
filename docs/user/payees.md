## Who an entry was with {#entry-payee}

Open an entry in **Activity** to see its payee: the person or organization the money was with. **Set payee** or **Change** lets you choose one, add a new one, or record that it's unknown. "Not recorded" means nobody has decided yet; "Unknown" means someone looked and the evidence didn't say.

Changing the payee never changes the entry. The amount, the accounts and the text from your statement stay exactly as recorded. When you change an earlier decision, Vult asks why and keeps both.

## Your payee list {#list}

**Settings** lists every payee with how many entries name it. You can add a payee, rename it or retire it. A renamed payee keeps all its entries. A retired payee keeps them too, but takes no new ones.

Two payees can share a name and still be different people or organizations. Vult never merges them because their names match.

## What agents do {#agents}

Agents link entries to payees from the evidence: the statement's description, the receipt, an earlier entry you linked. A similar name alone isn't enough, so an agent records "Unknown" or asks you rather than guessing. An agent with write access links directly; one that proposes sends the link to **Approvals**.

## Recurring charges {#recurring}

An agent can list recurring charges found from your payees: the same payee charging every week, month, quarter or year for a similar amount. Nothing is stored; the list follows your entries and payees, so correcting a payee updates it straight away.

## The pages {#pages}

- **Dashboard:** cash, income, expenses and net result for one period, with a chart of income and expenses, where the money went, and what you own and owe.
- **Approvals:** entries your agent prepared and wants you to check before they post.
- **Activity:** every recorded entry, newest first, with what it was for, the amount and its category.
- **Accounts:** every account's balance and activity, grouped into assets, liabilities, equity, income and expenses.
- **Statements:** the Balance Sheet, Profit & Loss, Trial Balance, General Ledger and Journal.
- **Sources:** the original receipts, invoices and statements kept with the Book.
- **Settings:** the Book's name, logo, accounting details, legal identity and address, your data downloads and connected agents.

## Switch Books {#switch-books}

Use the Book menu at the top of the page to move between Books. Each Book keeps its own accounts, entries, sources and Dashboard period. Check the Book's name before you approve anything.

## Choose a period {#choose-a-period}

The date range at the top of the Dashboard sets the period for every figure on the page. Step through months, quarters or years with the arrows, or choose exact dates. **Last 30 days** includes today. Vult remembers the period you chose for each Book.

## Follow an entry {#follow-an-entry}

Open any entry in Activity to see its lines, its dates, who recorded it and the originals that support it. Filter Activity by income or expenses, by account or by dates, or use **Search** to find entries, accounts and documents by name, memo, date or exact amount.

## Keep the originals in Sources {#sources}

Drop receipts and statements into **Sources** with **Add files**, or let your agent add them. Vult verifies each file and keeps one copy of identical files within a Book. Each source shows whether an entry uses it yet. Originals are stored encrypted with the Book.

## Make it yours {#preferences}

Your settings, in the profile menu, hold your name, photo, light or dark appearance and number and date formats. Vult supports 32 regional formats and lets you choose date order, 12- or 24-hour time, digit grouping and how negative numbers appear. These settings are yours and apply to every Book.

Vult keeps a copy of everything before each upgrade. Once after an upgrade, it says so, with **Restore…** beside it.

## Restore {#restore}

In any Book's **Settings → Data**, **Restore** shows your latest backup: when it was taken, and the version it holds.

1. Choose **Restore this backup**. Vult says what you lose: everything recorded since that backup.
2. Type RESTORE and confirm with your passkey.

Vult puts the whole Vult back, every Book in it, and may restart on the way. Keep the page open. When it's done, sign in again.

If the restore can't finish, Vult is left as it was.

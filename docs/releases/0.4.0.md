# Vult 0.4.0

Vult brings the Book your agents keep into your browser. Open it with
`vult open` and sign in with your passkey.

- **Approve the entry you reviewed.** An agent prepares a journal entry or
  reversal for you. See the debits, credits and balance changes, then approve
  to post that exact entry or reject it with a reason. The decision and the
  posting are saved together, so a retry returns the recorded outcome.
- **See your Book at a glance.** The Dashboard shows income, expenses, what you
  own and owe, and cash by account. Open the chart's table to see exact amounts
  by period and account. Accounts shows activity by class, a balance check and
  balances worth a closer look. Switch between what you owe and receivables
  recorded as owed to the Book.
- **Move between periods.** Step through months, quarters or years, or choose
  exact dates. The last 30 days include today. Your Dashboard period is saved
  for each Book. Whole periods have short links such as `?range=2026-Q1`.
- **Follow the money.** Activity shows the recorded entry, who recorded it,
  which accounts it used and which originals support it. Known brands can show
  their logos.
- **Keep originals in Sources.** Drop in receipts and statements, then find
  them from Book search. Vult verifies uploads and reuses identical files
  within the same Book.
- **Check your statements.** Open the Balance Sheet, Profit & Loss, Trial
  Balance, General Ledger or Journal. See the recorded scope and coverage
  behind the figures, inspect contributing entries and download a CSV.
- **Make the Book yours.** Save your name, photo, appearance and formats.
  Choose from 32 regional formats and set date, time, grouping and negative
  number styles. Add the Book's logo or photo to the switcher, update its
  legal identity and address, review or
  disconnect agents, and download a Book archive, journal JSON or CSVs.
- **Install locally.** Ubuntu 24.04 on x86-64 joins Apple Silicon macOS.
  Both native installers use protected services and local HTTPS. Upgrades
  from v0.1, v0.2 and v0.3 preserve recorded Books and their stored originals.

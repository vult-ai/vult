# Vult 0.6.0

Vult 0.6.0 adds the accounting foundations: a chart of accounts with
structure, payees, a cash-flow statement, statement reconciliation and period
closes.

- **Chart of accounts.** Accounts can sit under parents and headings, be
  retired with the transfer of any remaining balance, and come back. A new Book
  can start from a Business or Personal starter chart.
- **Payees.** Each entry can record who it was with, and agents can list the
  recurring charges found from those links.
- **Cash flow.** A direct-method cash-flow statement sorts each cash movement
  into operating, investing or financing from the roles of the accounts on the
  other side. Opening balances are where cash starts, not money coming in.
- **Reconcile statements.** Agents import bank and card statements; Vult
  compares them with the Book row by row and shows what's matched, missing or
  outstanding. A statement counts as reconciled only while it is still fully
  explained.
- **Close and reopen periods.** A close is a recorded decision that stops
  posting into the period. Vult first lists what's still open: unreconciled
  statements, and income or expense entries with no evidence or no payee. A
  year can be closed after its months. Reopening takes a reason, and reports of
  that period show it, including their CSV exports.
- **Works on phones.** Every page fits a phone screen, from 320 pixels wide:
  Activity, Accounts, Statements, Sources and Settings were reworked, tables
  scroll inside their own frame, and controls are big enough to tap.
- **Dashboard amounts show by default.** Hide them with one click; a browser
  that already chose to hide them keeps that choice.
- **Never stuck while getting started.** Every step of the first-run screen
  that waits for an agent, or for your decision, offers Skip for now and opens
  the Book.
- **Clearer words.** Vult speaks to you without "your" on every screen, names
  any MCP agent rather than particular ones, says plainly when you've signed
  out, and draws the cents of large amounts once instead of twice.

## Upgrade

Run `vult update`, or the command you installed with:

```sh
curl -fsSL https://vult.sh | sh
```

Your Books, entries, sources, connections and sign-in stay as they are. 0.6.0
adds one database migration, `0007_accounting_foundations.sql`.

- **Account roles.** Roles now come from a fixed list: `cash`, `card`, `loan`,
  `receivable`, `payable`, `fixed_asset`, `investment`, `cost_of_sales` and
  `opening_balance`, each on the matching account class. Roles an earlier
  release accepted with the same meaning (`cost_of_goods_sold`,
  `other_income`, `other_expense`) keep working. If a Book holds any other
  role, or a role on the wrong kind of account, the upgrade stops, changes
  nothing and names what to change first.
- **Gross profit.** On a Book whose expense accounts use the earlier
  cost-of-goods role, profit and loss now shows Cost of sales and Gross profit
  above operating expenses. Totals are unchanged.
- **Opening balances.** Give the equity account that opening balances post
  against the Opening balances role, and the cash flow starts from that cash
  instead of counting it as financing. New starter charts set it already.
- **Backups.** Backups now use format v6, which includes the new records.
  Earlier backups still restore.
- **Closed periods.** `list_closed_periods` now lists reopened periods too,
  with every close and reopening.
